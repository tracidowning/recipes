ingredients:
- 4 tablespoons unsalted butter
- 2 cups diced onions
- 1 pound fresh button mushrooms, sliced (set aside 1 cup to sauté for garnish)
- 2 teaspoons dried dill weed
- 1 tablespoon Hungarian sweet paprika (see note)
- 1 tablespoon low-sodium soy sauce
- 2 cups vegetable broth
- 2 tablespoons cornstarch
- 1 cup whole milk
- 1/2 cup sour cream
- 2 teaspoons lemon juice
- 1/4 cup chopped parsley
- 1 teaspoon salt
- 1/4 teaspoon ground black pepper
- pinch of cayenne pepper
- For Garnish
- sautéed sliced mushrooms
- fresh chopped parsley
- sour cream
- crusty bread or popovers, for serving (optional)

directions:
- Melt the butter over medium heat in a 5 quart dutch oven or heavy-bottomed pot. Add the onions and sauté 3 to 4 minutes, until soft and translucent.
- Add the mushrooms and cook 5 minutes.
- Stir in the dill, paprika, soy sauce, and vegetable broth. Reduce the heat to medium-low, cover, and simmer very gently for 15 minutes.
- Whisk the cornstarch into the milk until smooth, then stir it into the soup. Cover and simmer very gently, stirring occasionally, about 10 minutes more.
- Stir in the sour cream, lemon juice, parsley, salt, pepper, and cayenne.
- Warm over low heat about 3 minutes. Do not let it boil.
- Serve topped with sautéed mushrooms, a dollop of sour cream, and more parsley, with crusty bread on the side.

From [Belly Full](https://bellyfull.net/hungarian-mushroom-soup/). Serves 6. Prep 10 minutes, cook 35 minutes.

I recommend adding cooked chicken meatballs to the soup.

Keep the soup at a gentle simmer the whole time, especially once the sour cream goes in. Boiling can make it curdle.

Use real Hungarian paprika; regular paprika doesn't have the same depth. Sweet is mild, or use half-sharp for more heat.
