ingredients:
- 2 1/2 lb beef chuck roast, trimmed and cut into 1 1/2-inch cubes
- 1 tsp salt and 1/2 tsp black pepper, for the beef
- 3 tbsp olive oil, divided
- 1 1/2 lb cremini mushrooms, cleaned and quartered
- 1 large yellow onion, diced
- 3 carrots, peeled and cut into 1-inch pieces
- 3 cloves garlic, minced
- 1 tbsp tomato paste
- 2 tbsp all-purpose flour
- 1 cup dry red wine (or substitute an additional 1 cup beef broth)
- 3 cups low-sodium beef broth
- 1 tbsp Worcestershire sauce
- 1 tsp dried thyme
- 2 bay leaves
- 1 1/2 lb baby potatoes, halved (or quartered if large)
- 2 tbsp chopped fresh parsley, for serving

directions:
- Heat the oven to 325°F. Pat the beef dry and season with 1 tsp salt and 1/2 tsp pepper.
- In a large Dutch oven over medium-high heat, warm 1 tbsp oil. Sear the beef in two or three batches, without crowding, until deeply browned on at least two sides, about 5 minutes per batch. Transfer to a plate.
- Add another 1 tbsp oil to the pot and cook the mushrooms over medium-high heat, stirring occasionally, until they release their liquid and brown, about 8 minutes. Transfer them to the plate with the beef.
- Add the remaining 1 tbsp oil, then the onion and carrots. Cook 5 minutes, scraping up the browned bits. Add the garlic, tomato paste, and flour and stir for 1 minute.
- Pour in the wine (if using) and scrape the bottom of the pot. Simmer 2 minutes, then add the broth, Worcestershire, thyme, and bay leaves.
- Return the beef and any juices to the pot (hold back about half the mushrooms to add later). Bring to a simmer, cover, and transfer to the oven. Braise 1 hour.
- Stir in the potatoes and the remaining mushrooms. Cover and braise 45 to 60 minutes more, until the beef is fork-tender and the potatoes are cooked through.
- Remove the bay leaves, skim any excess fat, and adjust the salt and pepper. If you'd like a thicker stew, simmer uncovered on the stovetop for 10 minutes. Sprinkle with parsley and serve.

---

Serves 6. Prep 30 minutes, cook 2 to 2 1/2 hours.

For the slow cooker: Brown the beef, mushrooms, and aromatics as above, then transfer everything except the potatoes to a slow cooker. Cook on low 6 hours, add the potatoes, and cook 2 more hours.

Leftovers keep 4 days in the fridge, or freeze up to 3 months. The flavor deepens overnight, so it makes a great second dinner or lunches.
